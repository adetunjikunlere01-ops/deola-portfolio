Introduction

I’m Deola Oderinde, an internal communications and employee engagement professional with 7 years of experience across corporate communications and project management. I turn communications strategy into well-planned, well-delivered initiatives that build an engaged, high-performing culture: clearly briefed, delivered on time and within budget, and evaluated against success measures.

I lead a team of communications analysts and project support and deliver employee engagement campaigns, manage vendor and stakeholder delivery, and lead project planning from brief to post-project review.

My architecture background taught me to design for people, and I bring the same user-centred approach to employee experience and engagement.

I’m tenacious, resilient and naturally wired to solve problems. I approach challenges with curiosity and stay with a problem until I find a way forward.
